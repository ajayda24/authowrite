# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want Authowrite to be a welcoming place for writers, readers and developers of every background,
language and experience level. We expect everyone to:

- be kind and patient, and assume good intent;
- give and gracefully accept constructive feedback;
- respect differing viewpoints, and other people's creative work and attribution;
- focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery,
personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, plagiarism presented as
one's own work, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers by opening a
[confidential security advisory](https://github.com/ajayda24/authowrite/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants who violate this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
